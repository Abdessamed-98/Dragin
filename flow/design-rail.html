Tool Rail + Unified Session — interactive mock

One session of files; tools are lenses over it. Try the flow:

  1. Press Remove Background — the 6 images process.
  2. Click Vectorizer (pen icon) in the rail — same files carry over as results, chrome & action swap.
  3. Click Cropper — same session, focus view (canvas + filmstrip).
  4. Click PDF — same session, list view. Action becomes “Images → PDF”.
  5. Hover a thumb → revert-to-original. Hover the dimmed Upscaler → why it's disabled.
